## Title

Hindsight — check your next draft against your published past

## Description draft

Hindsight is an agent inside a creator's studio that helps review a new script against an indexed video archive. It connects retrieval and draft analysis to browser actions, so evidence can be inspected alongside the work. The intended interaction uses the channel and script already open in the studio, identifies potential conflicts, and opens a supporting transcript moment. The creator decides whether to revise the draft or explain a changed position.

The event work adds a tool-calling agent, streaming transports, and CopilotKit integration over an existing transcript engine. The archived corpus and original studio are inherited components, not work created during the hackathon. The final description must match the behavior actually shown in the demo, and eligibility remains subject to the organizer's interpretation of the reuse rules.
